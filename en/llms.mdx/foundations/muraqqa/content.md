# Third Pillar: Muraqqa' (/foundations/muraqqa)



The &#x2A;*Muraqqa'** is the third of the seven pillars. See [Muraqqa'](/dictionary/muraqqa) in the Dictionary.

## Muraqqa' as a daily practice [#muraqqa-as-a-daily-practice]

Unlike the [Wird](/dictionary/wird) which has fixed times, muraqaba is ideally a continuous state that is also practiced in formal sitting. The Shaykh prescribes dedicated times for muraqaba — typically after Fajr and after 'Isha — but the deeper aspiration is that the state of inner watchfulness become unbroken.

## The inner teacher [#the-inner-teacher]

The Shaykh teaches that muraqaba, properly practiced, activates an inner guidance that begins to correct the murid from within. Rather than always needing external instruction, the murid starts to feel a pull away from what harms the heart and toward what nourishes it. This is the beginning of the *kashf* (unveiling).

## Stages [#stages]

1. **Muraqabat al-af'al** — watchfulness of actions
2. **Muraqabat al-ahwal** — watchfulness of states
3. **Muraqabat al-qalb** — watchfulness of the heart's movements
4. **Muraqabat al-sirr** — watchfulness in the [Sirr](/dictionary/sirr)
