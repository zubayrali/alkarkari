The Way

What is the Karkariya?

Sufi knowledge is the spirit of our religion and its highest degree. The path is clear — smoothed by the Beloved ﷺ, who gathered it into a single sentence.

The Way

“Belittle yourself, and give importance to your neighbor.”

The Guide

Shaykh Mohamed Faouzi al-Karkari — of noble Prophetic descent, Idrisi and Hasani. At nineteen, he left everything he had, and walked.

The Light

The path is the seeking of that light — until it is seen, not as a metaphor, but with the eye.

The Practice

Seven founding principles

By the grace of God, our way is clear and simple.

  1. 01 / 07 · Subha

    السُّبْحَة

    The Rosary

    Counted remembrance — the litany kept close, morning and evening.

  2. 02 / 07 · Siyaha

    السِّيَاحَة

    The Wandering

    Travelling the land for worship — relying on God alone.

  3. 03 / 07 · Hadra

    الحَضْرَة

    The Sacred Dance

    The gathering of remembrance — presence, proximity, the Name in motion.

  4. 04 / 07 · Muraqqaʿa

    المُرَقَّعَة

    The Patched Cloak

    Many fragments, one garment — humility worn on the body.

  5. الله

    05 / 07 · Ism

    الاِسْم

    The Name

    The dhikr of the Name of Majesty — Allāh — basis of all the Names.

  6. الخلوة

    06 / 07 · Khalwa

    الخَلْوَة

    The Retreat

    Solitude in darkness, seeking the Light — the cave of the seeker.

  7. سر

    07 / 07 · Sirr

    السِّرّ

    The Secret

    What is hidden between the servant and his Lord — carried, not spoken.
